# Commercial licensing

Konjugate Fintech Toolbox is dual-licensed: it's available to everyone under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE) (AGPLv3), and separately available under a commercial license for organizations that need terms AGPLv3 doesn't offer.

## Why dual-licensed

AGPLv3 keeps this toolbox and everything built on it free and open, including when it's run as a network service — anyone who modifies it and lets others interact with it over a network must make that modified source available to those users, the same as if they'd distributed it as a binary. That's a deliberate choice: it's meant to stop anyone from taking this code, closing it up, and redistributing or hosting it as a proprietary competitor.

It also means AGPLv3 comes with obligations that some institutions can't accept for their own internal use — a bank building proprietary risk models on top of this toolbox, for instance, is not going to want to publish those models' source just because AGPLv3 requires it once the result is exposed to other users over a network. That's what the commercial license is for.

## When you need a commercial license

You likely need a commercial license, rather than the free AGPLv3 grant, if you want to:

- Modify or extend this toolbox and keep those modifications closed/proprietary.
- Embed it in a product or internal system without being required to publish that system's source.
- Offer a hosted or network-accessible service built on it without triggering AGPLv3 §13's source-disclosure requirement.
- Get support, an SLA, or indemnification that the AGPLv3 grant does not include (it's provided "AS IS", with no warranty, per LICENSE §15–16).

If you're only using this toolbox as-is, or contributing changes back under AGPLv3 yourself, you don't need anything beyond the license already granted in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
