Sample files for Fintech Start (all figures are fictional, in millions).

institutions.csv                   Eight fictional banks, one balance sheet each. Use this with exposures.csv.
exposures.csv                      Who lent to whom (38 loans). Optional: try importing institutions.csv alone
                                   to see exposures estimated from the interbank totals instead.
institutions-with-problems.csv     The same banks with three deliberate mistakes, to see the error messages:
                                   a balance sheet that does not balance, a duplicate name, and a value that
                                   is not a number.

In Fintech Start: Your data > Choose file... for each slot, then Check my data.
