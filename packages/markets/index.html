Fintech MarketsLinks between markets, and what-ifs

Everything stays on this computer. Links found in past data are not a forecast, and the model is illustrative.

Start from your own series

Choose one file per series: a date column and a price or rate column. Konjugate lines them up on shared dates, turns them into changes, and tells you what it repaired or left out.

Or fetch series from the internet

Nothing is fetched until you press Fetch. Only the symbols you type are sent, to the site you choose, and the answer is read the same way as a file you chose yourself.

Which series lead which

Konjugate looks for series whose past moves help predict another's, in several separate stretches of history. A link that keeps its direction and delay across stretches is worth more than one that appears once.

Shock a series

Each scenario runs the model twice from the latest bar: once as it is, and once with a shock, so you can see what the shock changes through the links you kept.
