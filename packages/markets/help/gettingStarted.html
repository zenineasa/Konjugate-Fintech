Getting started

Five minutes from a set of price files to a what-if.

1. Load your series

On Your series, choose one file per series (a date column and a price or rate column), or press Use the sample series to try seven made-up ones. Konjugate lines them up on the dates they share, turns them into changes, and lists anything it repaired or left out. Nothing leaves your computer.

2. Look for links

Konjugate looks for series whose past moves help predict another's, in several separate stretches of history, and counts how often each link shows up with the same direction and delay. Steady links appeared in most stretches; Comes and goes and Unsteady links are weaker evidence. There are two lists: leads and lags (one series moves a bar before another) and moves together in the same bar, which is usually far steadier in real markets. Tick the links you want in the model, then build it.

3. Shock a series

Each scenario runs the model twice from the latest bar: once as it is, and once with a shock. Choose the series to shock; the results show how far the shock travels through the links you kept, against the run with no shock.

4. Keep it up to date

If your files are updated by another program, Keep it up to date reads them again, repeats the analysis and rebuilds the model, on demand or every few minutes while the window is open. The list of earlier looks shows which links appeared or disappeared.

Links found in past data are not a forecast. The model is illustrative and has not been validated by a practitioner. See Assumptions and limits.
