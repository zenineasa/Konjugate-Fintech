Getting started

Ten minutes from a question to a comparison with what really happened.

1. Choose your series

On Your series, Search finds a stock, fund, index, future, currency or cryptocurrency by name and tells you which kind each match is. Groups adds a whole set at once (sector funds, commodities, currencies, Treasury yields and more) or lets you tick the members you want. Two or more series in your selection can be saved as a group of your own, which appears first. Type symbols takes Yahoo Finance, FRED or Stooq symbols directly, and My files takes your own files. Everything you pick appears in Your selection, where you choose the bar size (daily, hourly or every five minutes) and how far back to go, by preset or by dates. Or press Use the sample series to try seven made-up ones. Nothing is fetched until you press Get data, and nothing leaves your computer except the symbols you asked for.

2. Check the data

Before anything is analysed you see each series on its own scale, with the range that will be used shaded. Drag the two handles to choose the range, or use a quick choice. Switch off any series you do not want. Hold back the last month or three months to keep recent bars for a comparison later: the model then learns only up to the last bar in the range, its as-of date.

3. Look for links

Konjugate looks for series whose moves help predict another's, in several separate stretches of history, and counts how often each link shows up with the same direction. There are two lists: leads and lags (one series moves a bar before another) and moves together in the same bar, which is usually far steadier in real markets. Tick the links you trust and build the model.

4. Look ahead

Shock a series to see what follows through the links you kept, against a run with no shock. Replay real moves plays what really happened to chosen series after the as-of date through the model, and shows what it expects of the others beside what they actually did, with a scorecard against a guess of no change. Project forward runs the model with nothing shocked and draws each path with the range past volatility allows, against what happened if you held bars back. Choose how far ahead to run.

5. Keep it up to date

If your series came from files or from the internet, Keep it up to date reads or fetches them again, repeats the analysis and rebuilds, on demand or every few minutes while the window is open. The list of earlier looks shows which links appeared or disappeared.

Links found in past data are not a forecast. The model is illustrative and has not been validated by a practitioner. See Assumptions and limits.
